Session Structure — What to Do Each Session

Work through in order (1→4). All sessions follow this structure — the progression changes by phase, not the exercises. ~20–30 min total.

① Glutes — Do First

  • Hip thrusts
  • Glute bridges
  • Single-leg deadlifts

Primary weakness linked to Nov injury. Always first.

② Hip Stability

  • Clamshells
  • Lateral band walks
  • Single-leg stance

Single-leg work. Band optional.

③ Core

  • Dead bugs (legs-only now)
  • Bird dogs
  • Plank variations

Core is a weakness — build slowly. Dead bugs: legs-only until consistently controlled, then add contralateral arm.

④ Calf / Achilles

  • Single-leg calf raises

Essential for carbon shoe use & marathon durability at Abingdon. Do every session.

Progressions by Phase

The exercises stay the same — sets, reps and weight change as fitness adapts.

Scheduling Rules

These protect against interfering with running adaptations.
🚫 Never same day as a hard run
Not before or after threshold, intervals, or the long Q run. In marathon block: avoid Sunday (Q1 long run) and Wednesday (Q2 quality session).
⏰ 6-hour gap minimum
Where S&C and an easy run are on the same day, separate them by at least 6 hours. Prefer easy-run days or full rest days for S&C.
📉 Reduce at peak, stop at taper
Drop to 1×/week during peak marathon weeks (DW5–DW3, approx Sep 1–21). Stop S&C completely from Kielder HM weekend (Oct 4) — final 2-week taper.
