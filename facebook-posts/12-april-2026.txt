Weekend Roundup - 12 April 2026 🏃💜

What a weekend! 15 RTR runners headed out across 8 different parkrun venues on Saturday morning, with the group spreading right across from Merseyside to the Lake District to the coast of East Yorkshire.

---

🏃 PARKRUN - Saturday

Clarence: Stephen Crowe flew the RTR flag at Clarence, putting in a composed run on what's become one of his regular Saturday morning haunts.

Crosby: Andrew and Alison Whitehead headed across to Crosby for a new venue this week. Andrew led the way for the pair, with Alison running strongly alongside him. Always great to see the Whiteheads ticking off a new parkrun together 💜

Heaton Park: The biggest RTR turnout of the day, with 6 members out at the group's home course. Dale Richards was first back for RTR, running with his usual Saturday morning consistency. Mark Taylor was in good form not far behind. Josh Young tackled the course well on a chilly April morning, while Jane Grantham also had a solid outing. Chris Grayshon put in another reliable effort, and Chris Chris rounded off the RTR pack at Heaton, getting his weekend started in the fresh air.

Muncaster Castle: Andrea Booth ventured up to the Lake District for a parkrun at Muncaster Castle - an undulating 3-lap course on tarmac and gravel tracks within the castle estate, with views of Scafell Pike along the way. A lovely spot for a Saturday morning run 💜

Newborough Forest: A brilliant morning for Michael Aiken over on Anglesey, finishing 1st in his age category on a stunning course that mixes sand, forest trails and fire roads alongside the beach overlooking Llanddwyn Island. A superb run from Michael!

Peel: Tracy Wroe had a fine morning at Peel, taking on one of the group's favourite local courses.

Pendle: Angela Pradena enjoyed a parkwalk at Pendle, heading out to a new venue for the weekend and making the most of the Lancashire countryside.

Sewerby: Lucie Tighe headed all the way to the East Yorkshire coast for a parkrun at Sewerby, taking in the cliff-top paths and hall grounds near Bridlington. A lovely spot for a weekend away!

---

🏅 RACES

Saturday 12 April

RunThrough Lake District Running Festival (23k): Julie Smith, Neil Naisbitt, Delphine Bugarel and Clive Koffman took on the 23k distance at the RunThrough Lake District Running Festival, based in Keswick. The route takes in the stunning scenery around Derwent Water and through the valleys below the Lake District fells, though this year's runners had to contend with snow and hail along the way - making an already challenging trail event even tougher. A fantastic effort from all 4 of them in proper mountain conditions! 👏

She Ultra (50k): Kath Biddle took on the She Ultra - a 50k women-only ultra marathon starting on the beach at Abersoch and finishing in Pwllheli on the Llyn Peninsula in North Wales. It's a brilliant event that celebrates women in ultra running and raises funds for cancer charities, and with snow and hail hitting this event too, Kath had to dig deep across 50 incredible kilometres. What an achievement! 👏

Sunday 13 April

Montane Howgills Trail 26: Chris Mihalyi headed to Sedbergh in the Yorkshire Dales for the Montane Howgills Trail 26 - a stunning but tough trail marathon across the Howgill Fells. The course takes runners through some of the most dramatic fell scenery in England, with over 1,000m of climbing on remote moorland and valley trails. And true to form this weekend, it was another very windy day out on the fells! Brilliant effort from Chris 👏

Mersey Tunnel 10k: Sarah Wilson took on the BTR Mersey Tunnel 10k on Sunday - one of the most unique 10k courses in the country. Starting in Liverpool, runners drop down into the Kingsway Tunnel for a 3k stretch under the River Mersey, emerging on the Wirral side before finishing along the promenade with views of the Liverpool skyline. A record 4,100 runners took part this year, and Sarah was right in among them. Great stuff! 👏

---

Snow, hail, wind, tunnels and trails - this group really does take on everything! 💜
