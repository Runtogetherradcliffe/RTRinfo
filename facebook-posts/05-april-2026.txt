5 April 2026 💜🏃‍♀️🏃‍♂️

17 RTR members were out at parkrun across 7 venues on Saturday - and a brave group of 7 took on Stage 3 of the Steel Cotton Rail Trail this weekend in some truly wild conditions. What a weekend!

---

BOLTON PARKRUN (230 runners)

Paula Crosland had a great morning at Bolton, running well in a field of 230 finishers.

---

BURNAGE PARKRUN (252 runners)

Fiona Case was first back for RTR at Burnage, putting in a solid effort among 252 finishers. Derek Case was also out, enjoying a fine parkwalk around the course.

---

CLARENCE PARKRUN (92 runners)

Neil Naisbitt headed up the RTR contingent at Clarence, with Stephen Crowe and Helen Naisbitt both out in good form in a field of 92 runners.

---

HEATON PARKRUN (809 runners)

7 RTR members tackled Heaton on Saturday - the biggest RTR turnout of the morning - with Dale Richards first back for the group among 809 runners. Andrew Whitehead and Kath Biddle both ran strongly behind him, with Nicholas Coleman and Chris Grayshon putting in good efforts too. Jane Grantham had a fine outing, and Angela Pradena rounded things off with a lovely parkwalk around the course.

---

PENDLE PARKRUN (78 runners)

Delphine Bugarel made the trip over to Pendle - a multi-surface mix of grass, tarmac, trail and stone paths at Holt House Playing Fields in Colne - and had a brilliant run among the 78 finishers.

---

PONTYPRIDD PARKRUN (465 runners)

Julie Smith headed all the way to South Wales to tackle Pontypridd for the very first time - a flat tarmac route through the beautiful Ynysangharad War Memorial Park on the banks of the River Taff - and came home in a brilliant sub-30 in a big field of 465. 👏

---

WORSLEY WOODS PARKRUN (606 runners)

Tracy Wroe was in great form at Worsley Woods, putting in a strong run in a field of 606. Chris was also out and enjoyed a parkwalk through the woodland course.

---

SOCIAL RUN - STEEL COTTON RAIL TRAIL, STAGE 3

This weekend saw RTR take on Stage 3 of the Steel Cotton Rail Trail - a long-distance route linking Sheffield's steel heritage with Manchester's cotton history through the heart of the Peak District, following the Hope Valley Line. Storm Dave had firmly arrived for the occasion, bringing hail, snow and howling winds straight off the peaks. Undeterred, Andy, Clive, Delphine, Kath, Paula, Rachel and Paul pushed through every bit of it and completed the stage in brilliant style. A serious effort in seriously tough conditions - well done all 7 of you! 💜

---

What a weekend for RTR - parkrun across 7 venues and a storm-battered Peak District adventure. Brilliant stuff all round! 💜🏃‍♀️🏃‍♂️
